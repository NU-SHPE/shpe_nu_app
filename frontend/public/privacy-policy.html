SHPE Northwestern App — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 23, 2026

This app is built and operated by the Society of Hispanic Professional Engineers at Northwestern University ("SHPE Northwestern," "we," "us") for use by our own chapter members. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and how it's handled.

Information we collect

When you create an account, you provide:

  • Name (first and last)
  • Northwestern email address (used to confirm chapter/university affiliation)
  • Birthday
  • Sex assigned at birth, gender, and pronouns (self-described, optional where noted in the app)
  • School level (e.g. Freshman, Graduate), major(s), and minor(s)
  • National SHPE member ID, if you have one

As you use the app, it also records:

  • Which events you check into and check out of, when, and the points earned for each
  • Which events you RSVP to

Camera access

The app requests camera access solely to scan the QR code shown at chapter events for check-in and check-out. Camera images are never stored, uploaded, or transmitted — the camera is only used locally on your device to read the code.

How we use this information

Your information is used to run chapter operations: verifying membership, tracking event attendance, calculating points, and letting officers see aggregate attendance for the events they run. Officers with elevated access (organizers/exec, admins) can see check-in records tied to the events they manage; only admins can see chapter-wide attendance data.

Sharing

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with third parties for marketing. The app's backend runs on Google Firebase, which stores and processes this data on our behalf as our infrastructure provider — it doesn't use it for its own purposes.

Data retention and deletion

We keep your account and attendance data for as long as you're an active member and for a reasonable period afterward for chapter records. To request that your account or data be deleted, contact us using the email below.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or requests to access or delete your data, can be sent to: shpe@u.northwestern.edu
