Northwestern University SHPE — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

This app is built and operated by the Society of Hispanic Professional Engineers at Northwestern University ("Northwestern SHPE," "we," "us") for use by our own chapter members. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and how it's handled.

Information we collect

When you create an account, you provide:

  • Name (first and last)
  • Northwestern email address (used to confirm chapter/university affiliation)
  • Birthday
  • Gender and pronouns (you can self-describe or choose "prefer not to say")
  • School level (e.g. Freshman, Graduate), major(s), and minor(s)
  • National SHPE member ID, if you have one

As you use the app, it also records:

  • Which events you check into and check out of, when, and the points earned for each
  • Which events you RSVP to

If you choose to fill out the Careers section, it also stores:

  • Your resume (a PDF you upload)
  • Expected graduation term, what you're looking for (e.g. internships, full-time), and your work authorization and sponsorship needs

Only you and chapter admins can see your resume and career details. Admins use them to put together the chapter's resume book for recruiting opportunities.

Usage analytics

On the web version of the app, we use Google Analytics (through Firebase) to understand how the app is used — for example, how many people visit, which pages they open, and how often members sign up, RSVP, and check in to events. This uses cookies and collects information such as your browser, device type, approximate location (city or country level), and the pages you visit. We don't send your name, email, or other profile details to Google Analytics, and we only look at the results in aggregate. You can block it with a browser privacy setting or ad blocker, or with Google's opt-out browser add-on; the app works the same either way. The iOS and Android apps don't use analytics.

Camera access

The app requests camera access solely to scan the QR code shown at chapter events for check-in and check-out. Camera images are never stored, uploaded, or transmitted — the camera is only used locally on your device to read the code.

How we use this information

Your information is used to run chapter operations: verifying membership, tracking event attendance, calculating points, and letting officers see aggregate attendance for the events they run. Officers with elevated access (organizers/exec, admins) can see check-in records tied to the events they manage; only admins can see chapter-wide attendance data.

Sharing

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with third parties for marketing. The app's backend runs on Google Firebase, which stores and processes this data on our behalf as our infrastructure provider. Usage analytics are processed by Google Analytics as described above, under Google's Privacy Policy.

Data retention and deletion

We keep your account and attendance data for as long as you're an active member and for a reasonable period afterward for chapter records. To request that your account or data be deleted, contact us using the email below.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or requests to access or delete your data, can be sent to: shpe@u.northwestern.edu
